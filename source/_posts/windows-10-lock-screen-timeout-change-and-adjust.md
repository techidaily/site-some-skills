## Windows 10 Lock Screen Timeout: Change and Adjust

![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f260d9a6fe.jpg)   
  
 Windows 10 will switch off your monitor and the lock screen will time out if no gesture is made after one minute by default.  
  
 If you don’t want your PC to switch off in such a short time, here is how you can change the default setting by yourself in just two steps.   
  
[**Step One: Add Timeout Setting to Power Options**](https://tools.techidaily.com/drivereasy/download/)   
[**Step Two: Change the Timeout Setting in Power Options**](https://tools.techidaily.com/drivereasy/download/)   
  
  
**Step** **One:** **Add Timeout Setting to Power Options**   
  
**WARNING:** [](https://tools.techidaily.com/drivereasy/download/) Faulty changing settings in Registry could cause irretrievable error to your PC or system, so it is strongly suggested that you [**back up your Registry**](https://tools.techidaily.com/drivereasy/download/) first before you make any changes.   
  
 1) Click the**Start** button, then type in**regedit** , and choose**regedit** .   
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f2d3974960.png)   
  
 When prompted with the notification, hit**Yes** to continue.   
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f2d5615490.jpg) 

  
 2) Then follow the path below: 

HKEYLOCAL_MACHINE\SYSTEM\CurrentControlSet\Control\Power\PowerSettings\7516b95f-f776-4464-8c53-06167f40cc99\8EC4B3A5-6868-48c2-BE75-4F3044BE88A7

![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f2e44c1424.jpg) 

  
 3) On the right pane, right click the**Attributes** option and choose**Modify…** .  
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f2efd31725.jpg) 

  
 4) In the properties window, change the value in**Value data** box from 1 to**2** , and then click**OK** to save and exit.   
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f2f4f9a063.png) 
  
  
 **Step Two: Change the Timeout Setting in Power Options**   
  
 Now we are to change the timeout setting in Power Options. This is a rather easy step, so please feel relax.   
  
 1) Press**Windows key** and**X** at them same time, then choose**Power Option** .   
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f312a659c2.png)   
  
 2) Then choose**Change plan settings** for the power plan that you are using now. 

 ![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f31642aa76.jpg)   
  
 3) Then choose**Change advanced power settings** .   
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f31d99f6fd.jpg)   
  
 4) The Power Options windows will pop up. Locate and expand the**Display** category, you should be able to see**Console lock display off timeout** option.   
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f32acc1829.jpg)   
  
 5) Now expand this category, then change the time to whatever minutes you want. Then click**Apply** and**OK** to save the change.   
  
![](https://images.drivereasy.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/img_587f32f4230f2.jpg)   
  
 That’s it. You have just changed the lock screen timeout for your Windows 10\.
